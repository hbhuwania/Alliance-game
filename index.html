How to play

• Every question is a challenge for points — answer fast, the timer bar shrinks and so does the reward.

• 🔥 Streaks stack a bonus: get answers right in a row to earn like an alliance on a winning run.

• 🎯 Secret Mission questions pay double. 🗡️ Veto questions are traps — read carefully.

• The last question is the 👑 Ace of the Week — worth the most points of all.

• Everyone playing in the same week gets the same questions, so the leaderboard is a fair fight. A fresh quiz unlocks automatically every week.

• Miss too many and The System eliminates you. Bank enough and you become Master of the System.
