How to play

• Every question is a challenge for points — answer fast, the timer bar shrinks and so does the reward.

• 🔥 Streaks stack a bonus: get answers right in a row to earn like an alliance on a winning run.

• 🎯 Secret Mission questions pay double. 🗡️ Veto questions are traps — read carefully.

• The last question is the 👑 Ace of the Week — worth the most points of all.

• 🧩 Today's Challenge: 5 riddles every day (same for everyone), 60 seconds each — a fresh set daily, and the points add to your daily total.

• 🔤 Word Code: unscramble 10 words against the clock — ramping up from easy to hard, just like the word task on the show. Each correct word unlocks a code segment, and the points add to your daily total too.

• Everyone playing on the same day gets the same questions, so the leaderboard is a fair fight. A fresh quiz unlocks automatically every day, and yesterday's #1 is crowned Ace of the Day on the home screen.

• Tap the green button at the end to challenge your WhatsApp group to beat your score.
